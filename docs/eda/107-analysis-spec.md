- **Population versus sample:** The denominator for a county share is 3,144 counties unless the signal's eligibility rule says otherwise. The denominator for a person share is the sum of `POPULATION` on the eligible counties. Published case and incidence floors use the published-floor counties as the denominator, and the 2,493 unpublished counties are a separate missingness count. They are not scored as zero and they are not inside the floor distribution.

- **Missingness rule:** Null case and incidence floors stay out of those distributions. A null SVI, uninsured percentile, population, or RUCC stays out of that column's distribution and is counted. Climate `VALUE_STATE` `OBSERVED` uses `VALUE_NATIVE_DOUBLE`. `ZERO` contributes 0 when the native value is null, and the native value when it is present. Any other climate state is missing, not zero. `INSUFFICIENT` tier rows stay in the sufficiency margin. They are not moved into `LOW`.

- **Exploratory or confirmatory:** Descriptive and prespecified. Edges, groups, outlier rules, and the keep rule were frozen before the distribution queries. No hypothesis test from #59, #106, or #116 is repeated. Histogram peaks are a description of bin counts, not a modality test.

- **Method / assumption rationale:** Exact statistics are computed on the raw numbers. Histogram counts use `numpy.histogram` on the closed-open intervals between fixed edges, plus an underflow bin and an overflow bin. The rightmost interior edge is included in the last interior bin. Counts are not assigned to bin midpoints, and no statistic is computed from those midpoints.

  Fixed edges:

  | Signal | Edges |
  | --- | --- |
  | Published case floor | 5, 6, 10, 20, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000, 2500, 10000 |
  | Published incidence floor per 100,000 | 0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500, 2000 |
  | Population | the `POPULATION_EDGES` already used by #122: 0, 10000, 25000, 50000, 100000, 250000, 500000, 1000000, 20000000 |
  | SVI percentile and uninsured percentile | tenths from 0 to 1, the `PERCENTILE_EDGES` from #122. If a column's maximum exceeds 1.5, divide by 100 before the edges and record the rescaling. Do not clip. |
  | Temperature, degrees Celsius | -40, -30, -20, -10, 0, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 |
  | Precipitation, mm | 0, 0.1, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250 |

  Unweighted quantiles use the linear definition shared with `PERCENTILE_CONT`: the index `(n - 1) * p` on the sorted values, at probabilities 0.01, 0.05, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 0.90, 0.95, and 0.99. The empirical distribution function at a histogram edge is the share of raw values less than or equal to that edge. Population-weighted quantiles are the smallest sorted value whose cumulative population share reaches `p`. The weighted mean is the population-weighted arithmetic mean. The weighted dispersion is the population-weighted second moment around that mean, divisor equal to the sum of weights. The unweighted variance and standard deviation use divisor `n - 1` and are labelled as that finite-population moment, not as a super-population estimate. Skewness is the bias-corrected moment skewness, `sqrt(n(n-1)) / (n-2) * m3 / m2^1.5`, with `m2` and `m3` using divisor `n`, for `n >= 3`. The mode is the exact value with the strictly highest frequency. When every frequency is 1, or the top frequency is tied, the mode is not estimable. The modal bin is the histogram bin with the highest count. Ties take the leftmost bin.

  Outliers, inside each group with at least 11 non-null values: the Tukey fence is below `Q1 - 1.5 * IQR` or above `Q3 + 1.5 * IQR`, using the unweighted linear quartiles. The MAD flag is an absolute robust z above 3.5, with the #122 constant `MAD_SCALE` (`1.482602218505602`) times the median absolute deviation from the median. A MAD of 0 makes the robust z not estimable. Flags are counts of review candidates. They are not deletions and they are not disease findings. A published floor of 5 is heaping from the privacy floor. A null floor in a state that publishes no county floor is that state's publication rule.

  A histogram bin is a peak when its count is greater than both neighboring bins. Two or more peaks is the multimodality description. Underflow and overflow are not peaks.

  Tier policy comparison, not a new policy: HIGH is stored percentile at least 90, MEDIUM is at least 70 and below 90, and LOW is below 70, matching `tier1-review-percentile-v1`. Recompute those three labels from `PRIORITY_PERCENTILE` and count disagreements with `PRIORITY_TIER`. Report the minimum and maximum `RAW_MODEL_SCORE` inside each stored tier. Those scores are the absolute values the relative rule produces on this batch. They are not a proposal to replace the percentile rule. Cross-tab `EVIDENCE_SUFFICIENCY` by `PRIORITY_TIER`, and the tier mix by RUCC and by Census region, joined to the current atlas only to name the subgroup. `INSUFFICIENT` stays its own margin.

  Groups for atlas numeric metrics: national, Census region, RUCC, and `HUMAN_STATUS`. Case and incidence distributions are computed only where the floor is non-null. Climate raw distributions are county-day, unweighted, by measure, computed in Snowflake. County-mean climate (mean of eligible days) is a second grain, labelled as a county summary, and may be population-weighted. Climate is not stratified by all nine RUCC codes in the raw day table.

- **Spatial / temporal dependence:** Neighboring counties are dependent. This run does not treat counties as an independent sample. The adjacency table records shared borders for a later spatial description. It is not a spatial model and it does not change a standard error, because no standard error is reported. One snapshot is not a change over time.

- **Adjacency:** Insert into `ONE_HEALTH_LYME_GAP_ATLAS_ML_LAB.EDA.COUNTY_ADJACENCY_107` (`RUN_ID`, `RELEASE_ID`, `FIPS_A`, `FIPS_B`) with `FIPS_A < FIPS_B`. The predicate is `ST_TOUCHES` on `TRY_TO_GEOGRAPHY(GEOMETRY_JSON)` for both counties, executed as an insert that returns no geometry to the client. Counties that do not parse are omitted. The statement text is not stored in `REGISTRY.QUERIES`, because that helper rejects `GEOMETRY_JSON`. The insert is skipped when the run id already has adjacency rows. If the statement fails or exceeds the warehouse limit, the graph is skipped and the distribution results stand. No external boundary file is used. A `GRANT SELECT` to `OH_LYME_PROD_READ` is attempted. Failure of that grant is recorded and is not retried with a stronger role.

- **Effect-size / uncertainty output:** Counts, shares, quantiles, and robust-z magnitudes. No confidence interval and no p-value. A cell with a count from 1 to 10 is suppressed in lab metrics, column-profile levels, and charts. Zero on a denominator of at least 11 is kept. The write-up may name counties and give small counts.

- **Profile and chart rules:** `EDA.COLUMN_PROFILES` gets one national unweighted row for each atlas numeric signal and for each climate measure's raw eligible values (mean, sample standard deviation, and the seven writer percentiles). `EDA.METRICS_LONG` stores the grouped quantiles, dispersion, histogram counts, outlier counts, tier counts, and climate county-mean summaries. Charts, aggregate only, seaborn, low dpi, each PNG under about 40 KB, with no county names: a histogram of the published incidence floor, a boxplot of that floor by RUCC, an ECDF of the published case floor and the published incidence floor, and a histogram of daily average temperature. A RUCC or bin with a count from 1 to 10 is omitted or labeled `<11`.

- **Small-cell and write rule:** Writers target only `ONE_HEALTH_LYME_GAP_ATLAS_ML_LAB`. Inserts are insert-only. A second invocation reuses the run id and does not insert another run, metric, profile, finding, or adjacency row. `REGISTRY.QUERIES` stores the non-geometry statements. County rows and climate county means stay in memory. They are not written to a file, to git, or to Drive.

- **Key caveat / stop conditions:** Stop if the release pin or the 3,144-FIPS gate fails. A climate query over 10 minutes is stopped and that climate grain is not estimable. Adjacency failure is a skip, not a stop. Do not claim that a tail county has higher disease risk. Do not treat the September tier batch as a score on the October 15 release.

- **Amendment before any distribution query:** If every non-null `PRIORITY_PERCENTILE` is at most 1.5, multiply by 100 before the 70 and 90 cuts and record the rescaling. Stored tier labels are not edited. This unit rule was added before those values were read.

- **Amendment after the native-double climate pass:** `VALUE_NATIVE_DOUBLE` is populated only when `VALUE_STORED_TYPE` is `DOUBLE`. On this release the temperature and precipitation rows are almost all `DECIMAL` or `INTEGER`, so that column is null and those rows were dropped. The numeric value is `COALESCE(VALUE_NATIVE_DOUBLE, TRY_TO_DOUBLE(TO_VARCHAR(VALUE)))`, still inside Snowflake. A `ZERO` state with both sides null contributes 0. Any other state stays missing. The native-double-only climate metrics on run `107-county-distributions-20261026T0404Z` are not the climate estimand.

After execution, **keep** when the pin and the FIPS gate hold, the published-floor split is 651 / 2,493, and the stored tier counts are 315 / 628 / 2,201 with sufficiency 651 / 2,493. **Revise** when those reference counts differ and the difference is not the already named September-versus-October release gap. **Stop** when the pin or the FIPS gate fails.
