# ML #109 county evidence coverage: registered specification v1

- **Spec/version and owning issue:** `109-county-evidence-coverage/v1`, [ML #109](https://github.com/Caraway-Labs/one-health-lyme-gap-atlas-machine-learning/issues/109), parent [ML #22](https://github.com/Caraway-Labs/one-health-lyme-gap-atlas-machine-learning/issues/22). Follows [analysis-spec v1](../methodology/analysis-spec-v1.md). Matthew Caraway dispatched implementation of #109 on 2026-10-25. No other epic story is in scope.
- **Question / decision / allowed interpretation:** What is covered, missing, censored, or vintage-mismatched in each published county signal, and do the 13 October 2026 coverage figures in epic #22 section 4.1 reproduce on the pinned release? The decision is keep, revise, stop, or follow-up for analyst review of evidence readiness. A status is a surveillance or source-coverage signal. It is not disease risk, incidence, biological absence, a negative finding, or a training label. `No records` and `Unknown` are not negatives. A missing human floor is not zero. A published human count is a lower bound. Do not train on the Tier 1 score or on latent-class membership.
- **Estimand:** For each prespecified signal, the county count and share in each coverage state, at national grain, Census region, and (for the three status signals below) state grain. Joint human × tick × pathogen counts are the comparability partition. Source vintage windows are the time grain. No county-level list, score, or rate leaves Snowflake.
- **Unit / cohort / geography / time:** One row per county on the pinned release. The integrity gate is `COUNT(*) = COUNT(DISTINCT FIPS)` and a single `RELEASE_ID` equal to the pin. Include the District of Columbia, Connecticut planning-region FIPS, Alaska, and Hawaii. Census region is the state-group grain (Northeast, Midwest, South, West; Alaska and Hawaii stay in West). State margins use the two-letter `STATE` code. This is one snapshot, not a change contrast and not a forecast.

- **Missingness rule:** Do not replace a gap with zero, Established, Present, or a published floor. SQL null, `Unknown`, `No records`, `no_county_linked_record`, and unselected (no observation row) stay distinct and are stored as separate `EDA.MISSINGNESS` gap labels: `sql_null`, `explicit_unknown`, `explicit_no_records`, `no_county_linked_record`, and `unselected`. The heatmap uses one prespecified undocumented share: human `no_county_linked_record`; tick and pathogen `No records` or `Unknown`; numeric signals SQL null. A high western-blacklegged undocumented share is the species range, not an eastern data hole. Small cells use the writer rule below. A component count in 1–10, or its complement in 1–10, is not published, so a two-geography layer cannot be recovered by subtraction. A zero gap on a denominator of at least 11 is kept. Percentiles and moments use non-null values only.
- **Exploratory or confirmatory:** Confirmatory for the three section 4.1 headline counts and the vintage labels above. Exploratory for every other prespecified table (joint partition, region shares, state margins, numeric profiles). Epic #22 section 5 and [prod-exhaustive-eda.md](prod-exhaustive-eda.md) already described the October 6 release. Those figures are the comparison target. They are not used to drop a signal, change a bin, or reclassify a gap after the results are seen.
- **Method / assumption rationale:** Counts and shares. The atlas is a county census with explicit status levels, so a count of counties in each level answers the coverage question without a model, an imputation, or a test. Vintage mismatch is read from source metadata, not inferred from a correlation. `EVIDENCE_COMPLETENESS` is described as a derived column and is not treated as extra evidence.
- **Profile and chart rules:** `EDA.COLUMN_PROFILES` gets one row per atlas signal column except `GEOMETRY_JSON`, `FIPS`, `COUNTY`, `STATE`, `STATE_NAME`, and `RELEASE_ID`. `STATE_UNALLOCATED_RECORDS_2023` is profiled once per state, not once per county row. Numeric percentiles are `PERCENTILE_CONT` at 0.01, 0.05, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 0.95, and 0.99. Categorical `TOP_VALUES` keep only levels whose count is at least 11, and the whole list is dropped when the omitted remainder is in 1–10. No climate or land-cover value distribution. Charts, aggregate only: missingness heatmap of the undocumented share by signal × Census region; vintage timeline of the source windows; national coverage bar for human, tick, and pathogen statuses. Seed `1092026` is recorded. No draw uses it.
- **State margins:** `EDA.METRICS_LONG` county counts by `STATE` and status for `HUMAN_STATUS`, `TICK_STATUS`, and `BURGDORFERI_STATUS` only. Species, context, region, and the joint partition are national or region grain as specified above.
- **Spatial / temporal dependence:** Counties in one state share reporting rules. Do not report a p-value, a confidence interval that treats counties as independent, or a causal contrast across vintages. Region and state tables are the dependence display. The human year, the cumulative tick and pathogen window, the 2022 context layer, RUCC 2023, and any climate or land-cover window are not one time base.
- **Effect-size / uncertainty output:** County counts and shares. No interval and no effect size unless a share is the estimand itself. Headline reproduction is exact integer agreement with 3,144, 651, and 2,493. Any other difference from the 13 October narrative is reported beside the two release ids.
- **Small-cell and write rule:** Writers target only `ONE_HEALTH_LYME_GAP_ATLAS_ML_LAB`. `EDA.METRICS_LONG` uses `write_eda_results` (`n < 11` removes the count and the value, including zero). `EDA.MISSINGNESS`, `EDA.COLUMN_PROFILES`, `EDA.CORRELATIONS`, and evidence payloads on `EDA.FINDINGS` use the same database guard. A denominator below 11 clears that row's statistics. A positive count below 11 is removed. A zero on a denominator of at least 11 is kept. Correlation rows, if any later story sends them, drop the coefficient and `N` when `N < 11`. This story writes no correlation estimate. `REGISTRY.QUERIES` stores the statement text, its SHA-256, the Snowflake query id, row cardinality, and elapsed time. Result cardinality is not a geographic cell. Statements that mention `PART_TEXT`, `CONTEXT_LIMITATIONS`, `PAYLOAD`, `GEOMETRY_JSON`, or a limitations column are rejected.
- **Key caveat / stop conditions:** Stop if the context check is not `prod_select` pass, `scratch_write` pass, and `prod_write_denied` pass. Stop if the live release id or bundle SHA differs from the pin. Stop if FIPS are not unique or the release id is not constant on the atlas. Stop if a prespecified object is not readable; do not change role or read `RESTRICTED_CDC_*`, `RAW`, `GOVERNANCE`, `KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH`, or `CDC_HISTORICAL_VALIDATED_SNAPSHOTS`. Do not select `PAYLOAD`, `PART_TEXT`, `CONTEXT_LIMITATIONS`, geometry, or limitation text. A query still running at 10 minutes is cancelled and reported. Tier 1 and surveillance-situation outputs are not coverage inputs.
- **Result disposition:** **PLANNED.** After execution, set one disposition. **keep** if the three headline counts match and every other difference is a named vintage or release-scope difference. **revise** if a headline count differs and the source vintages do not explain it. **stop** if an integrity or access gate fails. **follow-up** is a later story, not this run: a multi-year human panel, national land cover, or a single time base. keep is permission to use the coverage table in analyst review, not permission to train or publish a score.

## Amendment v1.1 — alignment checks, before execution

These checks do not change the estimands. They were written before any coverage statistic. The disposition is **revise** rather than **keep** when any of them fails:

- SQL nulls of `CASE_COUNT_FLOOR_2023` equal the `no_county_linked_record` count.
- SQL nulls of `INCIDENCE_FLOOR_2023` equal that same count.
- `TICK_STATUS = Established` equals the sum of the two species `Established` counts.
- Census-region totals partition the county rows, and no bucket is `UNMAPPED`.

A release-pin or FIPS-grain failure remains **stop**. `GEOMETRY_JSON` may appear only in an `IS NULL` count. The geometry text is not selected.
